# Product Vision Evidence — 2026-09-16

**Status:** DRAFT RESEARCH NOTES  
**Purpose:** Preserve evidence used to challenge/refine Product Vision. These sources do not become product decisions by themselves.

## Mobile / game design signals

### GameRefinery — Mobile Game Market Review, March 2025
Source: https://www.gamerefinery.com/mobile-game-market-review-march-2025/

Observed signal: hybrid-casual products continued appearing in high-grossing charts, while established midcore games continued changing collection/gacha/live systems. Use: evidence that simple/readable cores plus deeper meta remain an active design pattern. Limitation: chart anecdotes do not prove that a new character collector will succeed.

### GameRefinery — Mobile Game Market Review, July & August 2025
Source: https://www.gamerefinery.com/mobile-game-market-review-july-amp-august-2025/

Observed signal: Wittle Defender combined tower defense, roguelike and auto-battle RPG elements and reached the US iOS top-grossing 200 during the reported period. Use: example of hybrid core + RPG/character-development meta. Limitation: one title is not a product prescription.

### GameRefinery — Mobile Game Market Review, November 2025
Source: https://www.gamerefinery.com/mobile-game-market-review-november-2025/

Observed signal: examples included unusual thematic/core combinations and casualized strategy layered with character collection. Use: supports testing distinctive core/theme combinations rather than assuming a conventional gacha battler is sufficient.

### GameRefinery — Mobile Game Market Review, December 2025
Source: https://www.gamerefinery.com/mobile-game-market-review-december-2025/

Observed signal: hybrid-casual approaches continued across genres; examples combined recognizable/simple core mechanics with deeper meta. Use: reinforces that meta systems should support a clear core rather than replace it.

## Physical / maker signals

### Bambu Lab — A1 mini specification
Source: https://cdn1.bambulab.com/documentation/quick-start-f507128172bdf/Quick%20start%20guide%20-%20A1%20mini-EN.pdf

Relevant facts: FDM printer; build volume 180 × 180 × 180 mm; included 0.4 mm nozzle; optional 0.2/0.6/0.8 mm nozzles; PLA/PETG/TPU/PVA listed as ideal materials. Use: real physical boundary if A1-mini compatibility remains a project target.

### MakerWorld — Commercial License Membership Agreement
Source: https://makerworld.com/creator-membership-agreement

Relevant facts: eligible creators may offer subscription/commercial-license models under the program; creators are responsible for ensuring uploaded/licensed content is original/authorized and that necessary rights/permissions exist. Use: demonstrates a plausible distribution/business path for printable assets and reinforces rights/provenance requirements.

## Interpretation

Evidence supports **testing** two things, not choosing them blindly:
1. a clear/simple interactive core can be combined with deeper character/meta systems;
2. original printable characters have a real creator/distribution ecosystem.

It does not establish that NOVA TAILS should be a gacha game, a MakerWorld-first product, or a multi-output product. Those remain hypotheses requiring behavioral tests.
