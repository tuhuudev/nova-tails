# P03 — NT-001 Recognition & Recall Protocol

Status: READY TO RUN
Version: 0.1
Date: 2026-09-16

## Research question
Does NT-001 have memorable visual identity, and which features drive recognition without prompting?

This is directional prototype research, not statistical market validation.

## Inputs
Use one current NT-001 candidate image that clearly shows the full Stage-1 character. Do not show the Character Bible, labels, names, element, skills, or lore.

## Respondent target
Minimum useful directional target: 3–5 independent respondents if practical. More is better. If only one respondent is available, record it but do not generalize.

Avoid teaching respondents the intended signature features before the test.

## Procedure

### Step 1 — Exposure
Show the character for 10 seconds.
Say only: "Hãy nhìn nhân vật này trong khoảng 10 giây."

### Step 2 — Unprompted recall
Hide the image.
Ask:
1. "Bạn nhớ những đặc điểm nào của nhân vật?"
2. "Nếu phải mô tả nhân vật này cho người khác mà không có hình, bạn sẽ mô tả thế nào?"

Record exact concepts before asking anything else.

### Step 3 — Identity perception
Ask:
3. "Điểm nào làm bạn dễ nhận ra nhân vật này nhất?"
4. "Nó làm bạn liên tưởng đến nhân vật/con vật/IP nào khác không? Vì sao?"
5. "Có chi tiết nào trông quá quen thuộc hoặc generic không?"

### Step 4 — Feature check (prompted only after recall)
Ask whether they remember:
- ears
- forehead mark
- tail

For each: ask them to describe shape/color without showing the image again.

### Step 5 — Re-exposure
Show the same image again.
Ask:
6. "Sau khi xem lại, có chi tiết quan trọng nào lúc nãy bạn quên không?"
7. "Nếu chỉ được giữ lại 3 đặc điểm để nhân vật vẫn là nhân vật này, bạn giữ gì?"

### Optional Step 6 — Silhouette recognition
When a proper distractor sheet exists, show several unlabeled silhouettes and ask which one is NT-001. Do not run this step with misleading or low-quality distractors merely to produce a score.

## Analysis
Track counts only when sample size permits; preserve raw responses.

Current design hypotheses:
H1 — Large triangular ears contribute to recognition.
H2 — Forehead diamond contributes to recognition.
H3 — Split flame-shaped tail contributes strongly to recognition.
H4 — Fire fox/canine familiarity may cause association with existing creature/IP archetypes.

Possible outcomes:
- H1/H2/H3 repeatedly recalled: preserve as identity anchors.
- Tail repeatedly missed: strengthen silhouette/topology rather than adding arbitrary decoration.
- Forehead mark missed but recognized after prompting: it may be secondary rather than primary silhouette DNA.
- Strong repeated existing-IP association: investigate differentiation before scaling the character library.

## Decision gate
P03 does NOT canonize the character.

Use results to decide whether NT-001 proceeds unchanged into detailed 3D, receives a small Visual DNA correction, or needs a stronger differentiation experiment.

Do not redesign based on one person's aesthetic preference alone.
