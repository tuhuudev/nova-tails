# NOVA TAILS — Product Vision v0.1

**Status:** REVIEW REQUIRED  
**Rule:** Statements marked `HYPOTHESIS` are intentionally not canon until reviewed.

## Vision statement

**HYPOTHESIS**

NOVA TAILS is an original collectible-character universe built around small, distinctive heroes and large adventures. Its first interactive product is intended to be an accessible character-collection RPG/card-battler with strategic team building, cultivation/evolution progression, memorable bosses, and character designs that can also become modular FDM-printable collectibles.

The physical collectible layer should strengthen attachment to the IP, not dictate every gameplay decision.

## Core fantasy

**HYPOTHESIS**

> Discover unusual beings from fractured worlds, build relationships with a growing roster, develop them through meaningful paths, form synergistic teams, defeat creatures and guardians shaped by each world, and optionally bring favorite characters into the physical world as printable collectibles.

## Product pillars

### 1. Characters worth caring about
Characters must differ in silhouette, personality, lore, gameplay and evolution—not merely rarity or recolor.

### 2. Easy to understand, meaningful to master
The battle model should be approachable while team composition, timing, build choices, enemy mechanics and boss phases create depth.

### 3. Progression changes possibilities
Cultivation, evolution and skill choices should unlock mechanics/choices, not only inflate numbers.

### 4. One IP, many outputs
Canonical character identity should support game, cards/portraits/content and printable collectibles without becoming separate inconsistent designs.

### 5. Production discipline
A small validated roster and vertical slice come before mass content generation.

## Candidate player experience — HYPOTHESIS

A session should broadly feel like:

```text
Choose objective
  ↓
Build / adjust team
  ↓
Battle stage
  ↓
Understand enemy mechanic
  ↓
Earn targeted rewards
  ↓
Improve / specialize character
  ↓
Challenge harder content / boss
  ↓
Unlock new strategic possibility
```

Character acquisition sits beside this loop rather than replacing it.

## Product boundaries for first validation

The first playable should NOT attempt:

- open-world exploration;
- MMORPG systems;
- real-time action combat;
- complex fully-3D gameplay;
- competitive PvP balancing;
- guild wars;
- player trading;
- blockchain/NFT systems;
- dozens of progression currencies;
- 100 launch characters;
- hundreds of finished printable models.

## Physical collectible promise

The project should test whether a canonical character can successfully become:

1. recognizable 2D/card art;
2. a coherent game unit;
3. a simplified modular 3D figure;
4. a practical multi-color-by-parts FDM print without requiring AMS for the base design.

This promise is not considered proven until real physical prototypes are printed and assembled.

## AI policy

AI may assist with research, exploration, concept iteration, structured-data drafting, balancing analysis, prompt generation and asset ideation.

AI output is not automatically canon. Canon requires review and repository update.

## Critical unanswered product questions

These must be answered before Product Vision can become `APPROVED`:

1. Who is the primary player: casual collector, RPG optimizer, idle/gacha player, maker/collector, or a deliberate combination?
2. What is the primary platform for the first playable/product?
3. What is the intended battle interaction: turn-based manual, semi-auto, or auto with manual ultimates?
4. What should make battle #100 meaningfully different from battle #10?
5. Is gacha essential to the product identity, or merely one possible acquisition model?
6. How strongly should cultivation terminology/mechanics influence the world versus remaining a progression metaphor?
7. Is 3D printing a core product pillar at launch, a community/marketing extension, or a later monetizable collectible layer?
8. What is the intended business model, if any, for the first public version?
9. Which age/market/content-rating constraints should the visual and monetization design respect?
10. What would make us cancel or radically change the game direction after the vertical slice?

## Success criteria for Architecture v0.1

We can explain, without relying on a large content quantity:

- who the product is for;
- what the player repeatedly does;
- why team building is interesting;
- why characters are collectible/desirable;
- why progression matters;
- how world, gameplay and visual identity reinforce each other;
- how one character can consistently become game + 2D + 3D;
- what the first vertical slice must prove.
