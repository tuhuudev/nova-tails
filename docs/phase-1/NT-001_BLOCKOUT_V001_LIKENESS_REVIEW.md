# NT-001 Blockout v001 — Likeness Review

**Status:** FAIL / deprecated on 2026-09-16  
**Reviewed asset:** `NT001-3D-BLOCKOUT-001`  
**Review source:** explicit project feedback against the selected NT-001 design references.

## Decision

`NT001_BLOCKOUT_v001.blend` is not an acceptable interpretation of the selected
Pyrox design. It is retained only as a historical technical-contract experiment.
It must not be used as the parent of a sculpt, runtime asset, or print asset.

## Why it failed

The model satisfies its named-object and camera requirements, but resolves them
as generic primitives rather than the visual language established by
`NT001-PRODREF-001` and `NT001-CONCEPT-SELECTED-002`.

Specifically, v001 fails to carry:

- the compact, white, layered-fur body and head mass;
- broad swept flame ears with physical inner flame/fur layering;
- large expressive eye and muzzle relationship;
- cheek/chest fur silhouette and short, separated standing legs;
- the large rear flame-tail envelope, with two lobes that read as a tail rather
  than generic paired tubes.

## Requirements for the replacement blockout

The replacement begins as `NT001-3D-BLOCKOUT-002` and remains a candidate until
a new projection *and likeness* review. It must:

1. use the selected production reference as a qualitative likeness target;
2. establish the Pyrox silhouette in clay before relying on color or FX;
3. retain the locked anchors `EAR_SHAPE`, `FOREHEAD_MARK`, and `TAIL_SPLIT`;
4. keep one tail root with two persistent physical flame lobes and no wings;
5. show the compact stage-1 proportions, layered head/chest fur masses and
   separated four-paw stance in the required eight-view render set.

This review does not promote the candidate references to canon or authoritative
measured orthography.
