# NOVA TAILS — Character Identity Briefs for Experiment v0.1

**Status:** DRAFT / EXPERIMENT ONLY  
**Date:** 2026-09-16

These are test stimuli specifications, not production canon. Names/species/factions are deliberately temporary. The four briefs maximize contrast so the experiment can reveal whether the design language produces identities rather than recolors.

## Shared test constraints

All four should feel compatible with one broad stylized sci-fi/fantasy universe without sharing the same body template.

Every concept must have:
- one dominant silhouette idea;
- one signature feature readable without color;
- a distinct body archetype/posture;
- a distinct shape-language emphasis;
- a distinct emotional/behavioral read;
- no more than 2–3 secondary motifs;
- enough simplification that the design remains legible at thumbnail size.

Do **not** add faction logos, rarity frames, text labels, complex weapons or excessive VFX just to create difference.

---

## Concept A — SCOUT / “MOMO candidate”

**Purpose in test:** establish the small agile pole of the identity space.

**Body archetype:** compact/light, large head but not infant-proportioned, narrow torso, springy legs.  
**Posture:** forward-leaning / ready to move.  
**Primary shape language:** triangle/wedge.  
**Secondary:** soft curve.  
**Signature feature:** a tail architecture that clearly forks/splits into a directional shape; it must remain recognizable in silhouette.  
**Secondary motifs:** tall directional ears; short scout scarf or equivalent directional cloth form.  
**Attitude:** curious, alert, slightly daring rather than purely cute.  
**Avoid:** generic robot-fox face, excessive visor dependence, tiny colored inserts, backpack clutter, Pokémon-like elemental mascot cues.

**Identity sentence:** “A tiny pathfinder whose entire body seems to point toward the next unknown route.”

**Contrast target:** should never be confused with B’s mass, C’s floating ritual logic or D’s asymmetric predatory read.

---

## Concept B — GUARDIAN / “BRIX candidate”

**Purpose in test:** establish weight, protection and stability without simply making A larger.

**Body archetype:** low, broad, heavy quadruped or semi-quadruped; center of mass visibly close to ground.  
**Posture:** planted / blocking.  
**Primary shape language:** square/block.  
**Secondary:** arch/dome.  
**Signature feature:** two large shell/shoulder masses that can visually close toward a protected center; silhouette should imply a portable fortress.  
**Secondary motifs:** short powerful limbs; one recessed central core/face opening rather than a conventional mascot face.  
**Attitude:** patient, dependable, immovable.  
**Avoid:** humanoid knight armor, standard turtle shell, generic mech tank, merely adding huge shoulder pads to A.

**Identity sentence:** “A living shelter that chooses where the battle line stops.”

**Contrast target:** unmistakably wider/lower than every other concept.

---

## Concept C — SPIRIT / “LUMEN candidate”

**Purpose in test:** prove the universe can support a non-mechanical, non-grounded identity.

**Body archetype:** suspended/vertical, minimal or absent conventional legs; body organized around a floating central form.  
**Posture:** hovering / composed.  
**Primary shape language:** circle/ring.  
**Secondary:** long taper/teardrop.  
**Signature feature:** a broken halo/ring system that intersects or orbits the body in a specific recognizable arrangement; the ring silhouette is part of the body identity, not detachable VFX.  
**Secondary motifs:** two long downward taper forms; small central face/light void.  
**Attitude:** observant, quiet, benevolent but alien.  
**Avoid:** generic floating fairy, angel wings, wizard robe, Pokémon ghost blob, particle effects used as the main silhouette.

**Identity sentence:** “A quiet being whose broken orbit seems to hold its body together.”

**Contrast target:** the only vertically suspended/ring-dominant concept.

---

## Concept D — WILD / “RIFT candidate”

**Purpose in test:** establish tension, asymmetry and danger while staying stylized rather than horror/gore.

**Body archetype:** lean predatory creature, long forebody/limbs with intentionally uneven mass.  
**Posture:** side-loaded / stalking, as if weight is always about to shift.  
**Primary shape language:** crescent/hook.  
**Secondary:** shard.  
**Signature feature:** one oversized crescent structure growing from only one side of the upper body/head/back, creating unmistakable asymmetry.  
**Secondary motifs:** long counterbalancing tail; narrow mask-like face opening.  
**Attitude:** controlled, wary, dangerous—not angry monster screaming.  
**Avoid:** random spikes everywhere, demonic horns + wings cliché, gore, Venom-like surface language, generic black-purple recolor as identity.

**Identity sentence:** “A predator shaped around one impossible crescent, always balanced on the edge of motion.”

**Contrast target:** only strongly asymmetric silhouette in the set.

## Cross-concept silhouette matrix

| Axis | A Scout | B Guardian | C Spirit | D Wild |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mass | Small/light | Broad/heavy | Light/suspended | Lean/uneven |
| Orientation | Forward diagonal | Low horizontal | Vertical | Side-loaded diagonal |
| Dominant shape | Triangle | Square | Ring | Crescent |
| Symmetry | Mostly symmetric | Strong symmetric | Radial/near-symmetric | Strong asymmetric |
| Grounding | Agile grounded | Planted | Floating | Stalking |
| Signature | Split tail | Closing shell masses | Broken body-rings | One-sided crescent |
| Emotional read | Curious | Dependable | Serene/alien | Wary/dangerous |

## Internal review gate before image generation

Reject/revise a brief if:
- its silhouette can be approximated by recoloring another concept;
- its signature feature disappears when blacked out;
- uniqueness depends mainly on accessories/VFX;
- it requires lore text to explain why it is different;
- it obviously reproduces the signature design language of a known character/property;
- it cannot be described in one identity sentence.

## Next step

Generate silhouette/concept exploration from these briefs as **experiment stimuli**, not canonical character art. Select variants based on the brief and cross-concept contrast, not simply the prettiest render.
