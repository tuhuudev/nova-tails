# Silhouette Selection v02

**Status:** REVIEW  
**Date:** 2026-09-16

## Objective

Convert the first visual exploration into a stricter silhouette test. This document deliberately ignores color, rendering quality, expressions and lore.

## Selection criteria

A candidate passes only when its identity is readable at thumbnail scale through:
1. mass distribution;
2. dominant orientation;
3. negative space;
4. signature geometry;
5. locomotion/grounding;
6. low confusion with the other experiment identities.

## A — Scout

**Keep:** compact low body + large forward ears + clearly divided tail mass.

**Reject:** silhouettes where the tail reads as one generic fluffy tail or the ears dominate so strongly that the body becomes a generic fox mascot.

**Required next change:** make the split-tail architecture readable as two purposeful geometric masses rather than fur tufts.

## B — Guardian

**Keep:** broad low mass + shell volume visibly larger than head/limbs.

**Reject:** generic bear/turtle silhouettes where the shell could be interpreted simply as a backpack.

**Required next change:** introduce visible shell segmentation/closure geometry in silhouette, with a recognizable protected/closed state concept.

## C — Spirit

**Keep:** floating central core/body + broken orbital negative space + no grounded quadruped read.

**Reject:** candidates that become a normal floating animal with decorative rings.

**Required next change:** make orbit architecture structurally necessary to the silhouette; the identity should remain recognizable if face details disappear.

## D — Wild — revision required

The second exploration still converged toward a pointed-ear mammalian quadruped. This fails the intended category-separation goal.

**Do not select the polished Wild render as a test stimulus.**

Retain only these abstract constraints:
- lean;
- asymmetric;
- crescent;
- side-loaded mass;
- wary/dangerous;
- one dominant unilateral signature.

Replace mammalian fox/wolf anatomy with a clearly different body architecture. Preferred next exploration space: **raptor/insectoid hybrid** with two powerful hind limbs, smaller asymmetric forelimbs/wing-blades and a unilateral crescent carapace/tail structure. Avoid recognizable dinosaur franchise anatomy and avoid copying a specific real insect.

## Internal confusion matrix target

Before external testing, each selected silhouette should be describable without color/name as:
- Scout → small forward explorer with split-tail geometry;
- Guardian → broad protector with closing shell;
- Spirit → floating broken-orbit being;
- Wild → lean asymmetric raptor/insectoid with unilateral crescent.

If descriptions collapse to species only (fox, bear, cat, dinosaur), the identity is not distinctive enough.

## Decision

The experiment remains valid, but the generated art exposed a recurring AI convergence problem: prompts describing emotional archetypes tend to fall back to familiar animal templates. Therefore future generation must constrain **body architecture before species styling**.

Next generation order:

`body topology → silhouette → signature geometry → neutral concept → color/material → personality details`

not:

`species → cute/cool style → accessories → recolor`.

## Gate

Do not begin participant testing until Wild has a non-mammalian silhouette and all four identities pass an internal thumbnail/confusion review.
