# Character Desirability Pilot — P01 Exploratory Result

**Evidence level:** EXPLORATORY ONLY (`n=1`)  
**Date:** 2026-09-16

This record preserves one real participant session. It must not be represented as directional market validation and must not be duplicated into synthetic participants.

## Silhouette responses

- Stimulus 1: perceived as a dark/shadow-like creature; associated with Eevee/Pokémon.
- Stimulus 2: perceived as another dark/shadow-like creature with a larger/heavier body.
- Stimulus 3: participant could not identify a normal creature form; inferred psychic/spiritual/object-control qualities.
- Stimulus 4: perceived as lean, speed-oriented / assassin-like.

## Unaided recall

Participant grouped stimuli 1 and 2 as similar and interpreted stimulus 2 as potentially an evolved/larger form of stimulus 1. Participant summarized the set approximately as normal/base, defense, spiritual and speed-oriented roles.

## Neutral-render response

- Wanted to learn more about stimulus 4 because it appeared powerful/high-damage.
- Considered stimulus 3 most different because it did not look like a normal animal.
- Considered stimulus 1 most familiar and associated it with Pokémon.
- Felt the four could share one world due to visual/color similarities.
- Expressed interest in playing through **combination and upgrading**.

## Internal interpretation — not participant quotation

Signals worth preserving:
- Scout/Guardian separation is insufficient for this participant and deserves redesign/retest.
- Scout familiarity/IP association is a differentiation warning, not a legal conclusion.
- Spirit achieved strong category separation in this session.
- Wild communicated speed/damage fantasy and generated the strongest stated curiosity in this session.
- Combination/upgrading becomes an interaction hypothesis, not a game-product decision.

## Boundary

No character is promoted to production canon from P01. No population-level preference, market demand or product choice can be inferred from `n=1`.
